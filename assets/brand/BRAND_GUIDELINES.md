# WebSystemBuilders Brand Guide

## Approved direction

The approved identity uses the supplied interlocking `WS` mark in blue and dark navy.

## Assets

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| `websystembuilders-logo.png` | Approved production logo and browser identity |
| `websystembuilders-mascot.png` | Approved abstract WS companion for branded illustrations |

## Usage

- Use the transparent artwork directly without a backing tile.
- Maintain sufficient surrounding contrast on both light and dark surfaces.
- Preserve clear space around the logo.
- Keep the logo flat. Do not add glow, shadows, bevels, or animation to the mark itself.
- Do not rotate, stretch, rearrange, recolor, or redraw the supplied artwork.

## Mascot usage

- Use the transparent abstract WS companion as a supporting brand illustration, not as a replacement for the logo.
- Keep the mascot's blue, dark navy, white, and light-gray palette unchanged.
- Preserve its W-like footing, blue S-shaped channel, and small inset white status square.
- Keep the complete silhouette visible; do not add a face, limbs, props, text, glow, heavy shadows, glossy 3D surfaces, or generic robot features.
- Place the mascot only where it has sufficient contrast and clear space.
